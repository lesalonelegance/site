A private Moroccan hammam in Fez

Want to try a real hammam in Fez without sharing it with strangers? Our hammam is individual: one guest at a time, just you and the person taking care of you. Perfect for a first hammam experience, in a clean, warm and calm space.

  • Private hammam: one guest at a time
  • Steam and black soap (savon beldi)
  • Full-body scrub with a kessa glove
  • Rinse and relaxation
  • Royal hammam: plus a 15–20 minute relaxing massage

Good to know

Because the hammam is private, booking your time slot is recommended. Bring a change of clothes; towels available on request. You can add a facial, nails or a blow-dry in the same salon. We speak French and Arabic, and we'll do our best in English.

Frequently asked questions

Is the hammam private?

Yes. It's an individual hammam: one guest at a time, just for you.

How much is a private hammam in Fez?

At Le Salon Élégance, the private traditional hammam (black soap + scrub) is 150 MAD and the private royal hammam with a 15–20 minute massage is 200 MAD.

Is the hammam for women only?

Yes, our hammam is for women only, with an all-female team.

Do I need to book?

Booking is recommended since the hammam is private. Call +212 5 32 35 94 91 or message us on Instagram @lesalonfes.elegance. Open every day from 10am to 8pm.

Where is the hammam?

19, Rue Abdelkrim Benjelloun, Immeuble Naser, in Fez city centre (Ville Nouvelle), next to the Agdal district office, 1 minute from Avenue des FAR. A short taxi ride from the medina.
